\((\mathbf{q},\mathbf{p})\)

A point in phase space where \(\mathbf{q}\) represents the generalized coordinates and \(\mathbf{p}\) represents the conjugate momenta.
🔗
For this work, we will assume \(q=\|\mathbf{q}\|\) denotes the magnitude of the coordinate vector and \(p=\|\mathbf{p}\|\) denotes the magnitude of the momentum vector, respectively.
